# M4 PR5 — Compliance Service

## Purpose

The Compliance Service manages reusable compliance policies, controls and evaluations.

## Components

- CompliancePolicy
- ComplianceControl
- ComplianceControlStatus
- ComplianceEvaluation
- ComplianceEvaluationResult
- ComplianceService

## Rule

Compliance remains provider-neutral and regulation-aware but not vertical-specific. GDPR, NIS2, ISO 27001, AGID and sector rules must be modeled through policies and controls.
